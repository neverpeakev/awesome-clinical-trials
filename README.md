## Databases & Registries

- [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/) - US National Library of Medicine's database of clinical studies. The primary registry for US trials.
- [WHO ICTRP](https://trialsearch.who.int/) - World Health Organization International Clinical Trials Registry Platform.
- [EU Clinical Trials Register](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/) - European clinical trials database.
- [ISRCTN Registry](https://www.isrctn.com/) - Primary clinical trial registry recognized by the WHO and ICMJE.
- [ANZCTR](https://www.anzctr.org.au/) - Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry.
- [Japan Registry of Clinical Trials](https://jrct.niph.go.jp/) - Japan's clinical trial registry.

## Patient Matching & Recruitment

- [HelloStudys](https://hellostudys.com) - Free AI-powered clinical trial matching across 300+ conditions and 600+ US cities. Instant eligibility screening.
- [HealthMatch](https://healthmatch.io/) - Patient matching platform using AI to connect patients with trials.
- [Antidote](https://www.antidote.me/) - Clinical trial search engine with partnerships across 180+ patient communities.
- [CenterWatch](https://www.centerwatch.com/) - Clinical trials listing service and patient notification system. Operating since 1994.
- [Clinical Connection](https://www.clinicalconnection.com/) - Directory of clinical trials searchable by condition and location.
- [ResearchMatch](https://www.researchmatch.org/) - Free, secure, nonprofit registry connecting volunteers with researchers.
- [StudyFind](https://studyfind.org/) - Platform connecting patients with clinical trials through digital outreach.
- [Massive Bio](https://massivebio.com/) - AI-powered clinical trial matching focused on oncology.

## Trial Management Software

- [Medidata Rave](https://www.medidata.com/) - Industry-leading clinical trial management platform.
- [Veeva Vault CTMS](https://www.veeva.com/products/vault-ctms/) - Cloud-based clinical trial management system.
- [Oracle Health Sciences](https://www.oracle.com/health-sciences/) - Clinical trial data management suite.
- [Castor](https://www.castoredc.com/) - Electronic data capture and clinical trial management.
- [REDCap](https://projectredcap.org/) - Free, secure web application for research data capture. Used by 6,300+ institutions.

## APIs & Developer Tools

- [ClinicalTrials.gov API v2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/data-api/api) - Official REST API for querying clinical trial data.
- [ClinicalTrials.gov Sponsor Pipeline Scraper](https://apify.com/taroyamada/clinical-trials-pipeline-monitor) - Tracks ClinicalTrials.gov sponsor trial pipelines. Approx. USD 0.01–0.03 per query on Apify.
- [OpenFDA API](https://open.fda.gov/) - FDA datasets including drug adverse events, recalls, and labeling.
- [PubMed API (E-utilities)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK25501/) - Access biomedical literature and citations.
- [UMLS API](https://documentation.uts.nlm.nih.gov/rest/home.html) - Unified Medical Language System for medical terminology.
- [RxNorm API](https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/RxNav/APIs/RxNormAPIs.html) - Drug name normalization and lookup.

- [CDISC](https://www.cdisc.org/) - Clinical Data Interchange Standards Consortium. Defines CDASH, SDTM, and ADaM standards.
- [HL7 FHIR](https://www.hl7.org/fhir/) - Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources for health data exchange.
- [SNOMED CT](https://www.snomed.org/) - Systematized Nomenclature of Medicine - Clinical Terms.
- [MedDRA](https://www.meddra.org/) - Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities. Standard for adverse event reporting.
- [ICH E6 (GCP)](https://www.ich.org/page/efficacy-guidelines) - International Council for Harmonisation's Good Clinical Practice guidelines.

## How to Find a Clinical Trial

1. **Identify your condition** and any specific treatments you're interested in
2. **Search by location** to find trials near you
3. **Review eligibility criteria** carefully before contacting a site
4. **Use a matching service** like [HelloStudys](https://hellostudys.com) for AI-powered matching
5. **Talk to your doctor** about any trials you're considering
6. **Contact the research coordinator** at the trial site for more information
